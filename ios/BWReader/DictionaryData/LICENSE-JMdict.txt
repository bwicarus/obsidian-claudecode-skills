Changes made for BWReader
-------------------------

The pinned English JMdict release was converted into deterministic JSON shards.
Only written forms, readings, part-of-speech tags, English gloss text, common
flags, and JMdict sequence identifiers are retained. The data is normalized to
Unicode NFC and indexed by exact Japanese form or reading. No new dictionary
definitions were added, and EDRDG does not endorse BWReader or this conversion.

Conversion source
-----------------

The pinned source archive is the English JSON conversion published by the
jmdict-simplified project. Its immutable release URL and SHA-256 digest are
recorded in DictionaryData/manifest.json and in build_jmdict_dictionary.py:

  https://github.com/scriptin/jmdict-simplified

This attribution file must remain with copies or substantial extracts of the
generated DictionaryData directory. Smartphone and tablet applications should
also expose this acknowledgement from a Sources, About, or equivalent screen,
as required by the EDRDG licence statement.
