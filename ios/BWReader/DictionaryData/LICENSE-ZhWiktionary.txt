Reader Japanese dictionary: Simplified-Chinese meaning layer

Source project: Chinese Wiktionary contributors
Source extraction: Kaikki machine-readable dictionaries and the pinned
wty Japanese-Chinese Yomitan conversion
Source page: https://kaikki.org/zhwiktionary/
Pinned source archives and SHA-256 digests are recorded in
build_jmdict_dictionary.py and the generated manifest.json.

Reader keeps Chinese senses, examples, etymology and related-word metadata as
an attributed display layer. JMdict remains the authority for Japanese
spelling, reading, part of speech and inflection. The generated data is an
optional App-private download and is not
included in the IPA, browser extension, book attachments, Pi sync, or iCloud
backup.
