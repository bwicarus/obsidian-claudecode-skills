Reader Japanese dictionary: Tanaka Corpus example sentences

This optional App-private dictionary download contains a compact selection of
Japanese-English sentence pairs derived from the Tanaka Corpus. The source is
published by the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group:

https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/Tanaka_Corpus

The Tanaka Corpus is available under the Creative Commons Attribution 2.0
France license. See:

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/

BWReader keeps at most five deterministic examples per dictionary entry and
records the source sentence identifier. The generated data is not included in
the IPA, browser extension, book attachments, Pi sync, or iCloud backup.
